# Elective Compass

Helps BITS WILP M.Tech AI/ML students choose their 6 electives (2 in Semester 2, 4 in Semester 3) from their target role, up to two minor interests and an optional specialisation.

How a plan is built:
- 3 foundation courses for the role, picked from course content (handouts)
- 1 starter course per interest (up to 2)
- the remaining slots go to whatever adds most of what real job postings for the role ask for

Built from the Semester 2 and 3 course handouts, the semester plans and 261 job postings (October 2026). Always confirm your final choices with the official list at registration.
